## Pectin Hydrogel

### Hydrogel composition

#### 3.1 Chemical Composition

- Pectin is a natural, anionic polysaccharide primarily composed of α-(1→4)-linked D-galac-turonic acid units, extracted mainly from citrus peels and apple pomace.
- Generally brittle compared to alginate.
- High water uptake capacity due to hydrophilic carboxyl groups.
