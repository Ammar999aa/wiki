## Overview

Most conversations about synthetic biology happen in English, in labs and in academic journals. For much of the public in Saudi Arabia and the MENA region, the field remains unfamiliar, and sometimes misunderstood.

Our science communication work aims to bring synthetic biology into everyday conversation, in Arabic and English, through formats people already enjoy: films, podcasts and social media. We also work beyond our own team, uniting iGEM teams across the MENA region through the Tawasol Initiative so that the region's synthetic biology story is told together.

#### Event Structure

- **Mission-based crews:** on arrival, each attendee joined a themed crew, such as Biobuilders, Plant Rescue or Green Galaxy, and took on a science role within it, such as Robot Engineer, Space Pilot or Plant Scientist. This encouraged teamwork and mixed people who didn't know each other.
- **Synthetic biology discussion:** before the screening, attendees received a logbook with prompts on plant biology, space agriculture and ecological challenges. Afterwards, each crew discussed the film together, and we introduced real synthetic biology research, such as biodegradable plastics, bridging the gap between science fiction and active research.
- **Operation Earth 2.0:** to close, each crew sketched its own plan to rebuild Earth using synthetic biology, space science and engineering.

We had episodes in both English and Arabic depending on our guests and provided subtitles in different languages to cater to the community.

## Social Media

We launched KAUST iGEM's LinkedIn, Instagram and TikTok accounts and developed our content strategy together with KAUST's Global Branding and Communications office. Our channels serve Saudi and regional youth, professionals, sponsors and partner teams.

As part of our public science communication efforts, KAUST iGEM used Instagram, TikTok, and LinkedIn to share our research, document our team's activities, and introduce synthetic biology to audiences beyond the laboratory. Each platform helped us reach a different audience, from students and young researchers to professionals working in science, education, and biotechnology. Through short videos, educational posts, and event announcements, we aimed to make our work more accessible and encourage people to learn about synthetic biology.

## Tawasol: Uniting MENA iGEM Teams

### Overview

Tawasol (تواصل) means "connection." Every year, iGEM teams across the MENA region do valuable outreach, often in Arabic and often for audiences hearing about synthetic biology for the first time, but most of it happens in isolation. Each team builds its own glossary, its own infographics and its own surveys, and much of that work is lost when the season ends.

Tawasol began with the <a href="/kaust-ksa/education#tawasol-hackathon">Tawasol Hackathon</a>, which KAUST iGEM co-organized with NYU Abu Dhabi iGEM in August 2026. Building on that partnership, KAUST iGEM launched the Tawasol Initiative to connect MENA iGEM teams into a regional coalition with four goals:

<ol class="goals">

<li>

**Arabic synthetic biology education:** co-create accessible, standardized educational tools, glossaries and outreach materials in Arabic.

</li>
<li>

**Unified regional outreach:** co-design Arabic public engagement tools and culturally tailored science communication.

</li>
<li>

**Stronger Human Practices:** collaborate on bio-governance, policy and public perception in our region.

</li>
<li>

**A joint public showcase:** present the region's synthetic biology work together to local and global audiences.

</li>

</ol>

### The Tawasol Summit

On September 27–28, 2026, we hosted the virtual Tawasol Summit with regional iGEM teams.

- **Day 1, Kickoff and Vision:** teams introduced themselves, we presented the Tawasol vision and the case for Arabic synthetic biology education, and each team gave a lightning preview of its project and local outreach goals.
- **Day 2, Co-working session:** teams compared public perceptions of synthetic biology across their countries, audited key synthetic biology terms to agree on how to translate and simplify them in Arabic, planned the public showcase and its shared assets, and co-authored a joint regional statement.

### The Tawasol Public Showcase

Building on the Summit, the Tawasol Public Showcase, planned for October 2026, will bring the region's iGEM teams together to present their work to local and global audiences, with teams unable to attend in person featured through video.
