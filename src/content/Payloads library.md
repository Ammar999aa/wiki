1. **LeIBP Antifreeze Protein**

- Tested — expressed & validated in our platform
- Secreted antifreeze protein from _Leucosporidium_ sp. AY30, a psychrophilic yeast isolated from Arctic sea ice

**Function:**

Binds directly to the surface of forming ice crystals and inhibits further growth, limiting the damage ice formation causes to cells and tissue during freezing. Moderate thermal hysteresis (TH) activity, strong ice recrystallization inhibition (IRI) activity.

**Applications**

- Cryopreservation of cells, tissue and biologics
- Food and agriculture freeze protection
- Cold-chain stability for space missions

**Host organisms**  
S. cerevisiae, P. pastoris, B. subtilis

Structure \- [https://www.rcsb.org/structure/3UYV](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/3UYV)

7. **Sclerostin-neutralizing Nanobody**

- anti-SOST Nb

**Function**

Sclerostin is a Wnt pathway inhibitor secreted by osteocytes that suppresses bone formation. Blocking of this molecule can significantly increases bone density. Previously, sclerostin antibody treatment was used in mice exposed to partial weight-bearing, which simulated lunar gravity at 0.16 g. The treatment significantly inhibited skeletal deterioration and maintained cortical and trabecular bone parameters. Another example of a similar system is Romosozumab (IgG antibody), which was already FDA approved in 2019 for treatment of osteoporosis in postmenopausal women at high risk of fracture. Compared to conventional antibodies, nanobodies would have improved bone tissue penetration and easier production and purification process. Hence, we plan to work on anti-SOST development to further improve such a treatment mode in future. It would be especially important for space travels, as cosmic missions induce rapid bone mineral density loss that can lead to premature osteoporosis. Astronauts lose about 1%-1.5% of their bone mass every month in space, particularly in weight-bearing areas like the hips, pelvis, and spine. This is roughly 10 times faster than age-related bone loss on Earth.

**Applications**

- Microgravity induced osteoporosis treatment
- Postmenopausal osteoporosis treatment
- Osteogenesis imperfecta treatment

**Host organisms**

S. cerevisiae, P. pastoris, B. subtilis

8. **Follistatin**

- Myostatin Inhibitor

**Function**

Inhibits myostatin to promote muscle growth and preserve muscle mass. It is highly relevant to muscle wasting, especially in space since skeletal muscle atrophy is essentially universal in spaceflight. The condition affects every astronaut who spends time in microgravity, regardless of fitness level before launch. In particular, after long duration space missions, recovery of the muscles to preflight condition takes months to over a year after returning to Earth. For example, upon arrival on Mars, muscle mass loss is predicted to be around 25-40%, meaning that the crew may be too weak to even perform surface operations. Though astronauts exercise daily using resistance devices and aerobic equipment, it doesn’t fully prevent muscle atrophy. This is why protein therapeutics like follistatin are highly crucial for space missions. Moreover, FST induces a dual anabolic/anti-catabolic mechanism, making it one of the most powerful therapy options. In animal models, FST overexpression produces 2-4x normal muscle mass, which is the most dramatic hypertrophy observed due to protein treatment.

**Applications**

- Muscle atrophy treatment in spaceflight
- Sarcopenia (age-related muscle loss) treatment
- Duchenne muscular dystrophy treatment
- Spinal muscular atrophy treatment

**Host organisms**

S. cerevisiae, P. pastoris

9. **Interferon-β**

- **IFN-β**

**Function**  
Interferon-β is atype I interferon that serves as one of the first-line antiviral defense systems. It is produced by the majority of all nucleated cells in response to viral infection, double-stranded RNA, or innate immune pattern recognition. Essentially, this protein plays a crucial role in antiviral defense and modulation of immune cells. It also exhibits anti-tumor effects due to direct inhibition of tumor cell cycle progression, promotion of tumor cell apoptosis, and enhancement of NK cells and cytotoxic T lymphocyte. It can also play a role in neuroprotection as it reduces neuroinflammation by suppressing microglial activation. Due to such a broad range of applications, IFN-β will be highly useful in various space and Earth applications.

**Applications**

- Multiple sclerosis treatment
- Radiation cancer protection
- Antiviral treatment

**Host organisms**

S. cerevisiae, P. pastoris, B.subtilis
