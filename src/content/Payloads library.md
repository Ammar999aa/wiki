1) **LeIBP Antifreeze Protein**   
* Tested — expressed & validated in our platform  
* Secreted antifreeze protein from *Leucosporidium* sp. AY30, a psychrophilic yeast isolated from Arctic sea ice

**Function:**

Binds directly to the surface of forming ice crystals and inhibits further growth, limiting the damage ice formation causes to cells and tissue during freezing. Moderate thermal hysteresis (TH) activity, strong ice recrystallization inhibition (IRI) activity. 

**Applications**

* Cryopreservation of cells, tissue and biologics  
* Food and agriculture freeze protection  
* Cold-chain stability for space missions

**Host organisms**  
S. cerevisiae, P. pastoris, B. subtilis

3) **HSV Nanobody**  
* Tested — expressed & validated in our platform  
* Nb1\_gbHSV \- anti-HSV single-domain antibody


**Function**

Alpaca-derived single-domain antibody fragment (nanobody) that neutralizes herpes simplex virus types 1 and 2 (HSV-1 and HSV-2) by locking the essential envelope protein glycoprotein B (gB) in its unstable prefusion conformation.

**Applications**

* Antiviral therapeutics  
* Biosensors  
* Protein detection and imaging  
* Pull-down and co-IP assays

**Host organisms**

S. cerevisiae, P. pastoris, B. subtilis

5) **mCherry**  
* Proof of Concept — Red fluorescent protein  
* Tested — expressed & validated in our platform  
* A bright, photostable, monomeric red fluorescent protein

**Function**

Emits red fluorescence when excited by the appropriate wavelength of light, without requiring any substrate or cofactor. It was engineered through directed evolution of DsRed → mRFP1 → mCherry. Doesn’t have tendency to oligomerize, which is essential for fusion tagging

**Applications**

* Cell and tissue imaging  
* Protein and organelle tracking  
* Reporter assays  
* Flow cytometry and cell sorting

**Host organisms**

S. cerevisiae, P. pastoris, B. subtilis
